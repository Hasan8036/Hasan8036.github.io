About

I build things, then I measure whether they actually worked.

I'm a Master of Management Analytics candidate at McGill's Desautels Faculty of Management, with a BS in Computer Science from LUMS. The degree order is deliberate — I learned to build the system first, then went back for the language to argue about what it's worth.

That combination shows up in how I work. I'm comfortable writing the query, structuring the model, or shipping the product, but the output I care about is the decision on the other side of it. An entrepreneurial instinct with the technical depth to actually execute on it.

I'm looking toward strategy and AI consulting, and business and data analytics — the roles where those two halves stop competing and start compounding.
